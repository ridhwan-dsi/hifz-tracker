88 · Al-Ghashiyah Surah view
Only for surahs that cross pages. Start/End lines placed automatically from the 15-line layout; drag to correct. Crop top/bottom trims page margins in this view only. Tally here counts a full-surah read.
  • Bottom tabs pick which count shows on the right and the sort order; ↓ flips direction. No separate sort/filter button.
  • By surah: multi-page surahs stay grouped, ordered by their first page; split surahs show their page parts. By page: one row per page (per surah on shared pages).
  • Show all off: pages done for today and pages at their total target are hidden, with a one-line count at the bottom.
  • Surah view switch appears only on surahs that cross a page break; pages stay unchanged in normal view.
